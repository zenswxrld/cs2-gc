# All 36 collections: artwork and colour guide

The catalogue contains 576 visible gun skins across all 34 firearms, with 16 different weapons per collection and a 1 Covert / 2 Classified / 3 Restricted / 4 Mil-Spec / 6 Industrial split. Each donor finish is used once across the visible catalogue. New illustrations stay on their original weapon layouts. Lower tiers use simpler authored patterns and neutral hardware; the more detailed designs occupy the premium tiers.

The ten approved popular chase additions remain, along with Gold Brick and its black-grain wood, Berry families, Rainbow Spoon Deagle, Foil AK and the approved cyan Composite Armour AWP. The original Galil Rainbow Spoon and UMP Crimson Foil are excluded from new slots. No glove or knife collections change.

Selection uses [tracked player loadouts](https://prosettings.net/items/) and [completed-sale activity](https://www.steamanalyst.com/most-sold), checked 25 October 2026. These cover different audiences and are not a universal popularity ranking. Recognizable designs also extend to pistols, SMGs, shotguns, snipers and heavies: Kill Confirmed, Water Elemental, Ocean Drive, Dragonfire, Disco Tech, Chatterbox, Akihabara Accept, Hyper Beast, Starlight Protector, Power Loader and others.

The colourway review refines 101 slots in twelve pools with coordinated warm/cool accents; [review details](COLOURWAY-REVIEW.md) explain the Neon Rider families and Tropical Breeze overlay repair. Distinct validated exceptions and approved premium families remain.

Fixed skins use the collection palette, with protected black/white lettering and hardware. One or two premium colour families randomize on pulls (Undertow has three following the requested AXIA family); existing owned variants remain defined. Printstream inversions retain the source pearl material, and native glitter/pearl finishes keep their observed effects. Every collection has at least one active special material.

## Coverage and validation

The material preflight checks every slot against the actual supported-build assets, native weapon compatibility, distinct non-stock family colours, shared-pattern/palette near matches and complete tier counts. Migration preserves approved entries and falls back to the complete previous pool when a material is unavailable. Historical paint-kit names and IDs remain defined.

Full-resolution generated artwork uses native lossless LZ4 mip blocks. Archive packing streams numbered VPK parts instead of building the entire archive in memory; the standard VPK reader and directory signatures continue to apply.

Automated material and packaging checks do not replace mounted client review. Lighting, sheen and the final weapon appearance still need in-game inspection.
