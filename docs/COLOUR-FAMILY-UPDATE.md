# Colour family update

84 skin slots changed across all 36 collections. Ash appears in 11 colour
selections instead of 91. Cold Ignition keeps its approved silver/black Ash
variant. Other retained neutrals are deliberate hardware finishes or sources
that fail the visible-colour-change guard when given a chromatic treatment.

The family options now include more rose, crimson, amber, magenta, violet and
orange. B the Monster, Oni Taiji and Bloodsport have explicit main/accent ink
recipes for their reviewed colour variants, retaining native texture detail,
neutral lettering, alpha and material bindings.

Gold Brick, its black wood treatment, the Rainbow Spoon Deagle, the inverted
Printstreams and the existing bespoke artwork repairs are preserved. Existing
owned variants remain in the historical definitions. Newly rolled variants are
included in the native collection item sets used by trade-ups.

The migration accepts explicit colour-only revisions while still rejecting
replacement of a later approved donor, weapon, artwork or label. Native material
preflight passed for all 36 collections / 576 slots / 723 outcomes, including
family distinctness and the cross-collection similarity audit. All 36 collection
unit tests passed with the native texture decoder enabled.

The material checks do not prove the appearance under in-game lighting. Please
review the revised families in the client after installing. This update does
not change pity, rarity weights or add duplicate protection.

Close CS2 and its helper, extract the update into the existing portable game
folder, replacing included files, then launch normally. The launcher regenerates
the affected collection pack; the first launch needs that preparation step.
No player inventory, rank, configuration or Valve assets are shipped.
