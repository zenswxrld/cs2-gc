# Chase artwork shortlist and colour policy

Reviewed against supported build 25966348. No glove or knife changes.

Popularity is a shortlist, not one universal ranking: tracked pro loadouts and completed-sale counts cover different audiences. Research checked 25 October 2026:
- [ProSettings rifle usage](https://prosettings.net/items/weapon/rifles/)
- [ProSettings AK guide](https://prosettings.net/guides/best-ak-skins-cs2/)
- [SteamAnalyst completed sales](https://www.steamanalyst.com/most-sold)
- [Valve finish/material guide](https://www.counter-strike.net/workshop/workshopfinishes/)

The broader review includes Printstream, Asiimov, Bloodsport, Vulcan, Redline, Neon Rider, The Empress, Cyrex, Hyper Beast, Neo-Noir, Temukau and Inheritance. Supported source artwork and collection fit decide the actual batch.

Gold Brick remains Forged's Covert chase. Its four gold colours and Doppler smoke pattern stay intact. A private copy of the native shared paint procedure overrides only its base albedo, using black-stained AK wood textures for both native UV layouts. Normal maps, roughness, alpha and grain remain; other AK finishes never reference the private wood inputs.

Each family must produce at least three distinct non-stock materials. Unsupported recipes retain a complete prior pool and report the reason. New target weapons keep the 16 unique weapons and 1/2/3/4/6 tier counts; displaced family chases become fixed lower-tier entries. Owned historical kit IDs remain defined. In-game appearance and mounted composite behavior require client review.
